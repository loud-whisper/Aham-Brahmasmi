# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Discuss the work, provide evidence for technical claims and allow people to correct mistakes. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may moderate comments and contributions or restrict participation to protect the project and its users. Apply those decisions to conduct rather than a person's background or identity.

Use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive content. Repository moderation requests can be raised in an issue without private or identifying details. Security reports use the private vulnerability-reporting route in SECURITY.md. Do not post sensitive material in a public report.
