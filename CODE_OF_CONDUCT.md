# Code of conduct

Aham Brahmasmi adopts the standard [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
by reference, including its pledge, standards, enforcement responsibilities,
scope and four-step enforcement guidelines. The upstream policy remains attributed
to Contributor Covenant; this project does not claim authorship of that text.

## Project enforcement contact

The maintainer is responsible for enforcement. Report concerns privately to
aham1700@outlook.com, the contact designated by the maintainer. Do not post
incident details or another person's private information in a public issue.

This policy governs community participation. Framework source licensing remains
described by `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`.
